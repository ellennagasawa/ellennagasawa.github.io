I designed and authored this accelerated e-learning course to develop foundational listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills in Portuguese. Students practice personal introductions, daily routines, hobbies, food, shopping, and other practical communication through interactive exercises, recordings, writing tasks, and role-plays.

The course integrates cultural perspectives from Brazil, Portugal, and Mozambique. Its four-module structure culminates in a written introduction, a recorded oral role-play, and a short intercultural reflection.
