I designed and authored Portuguese Elements II as the continuation of the summer Portuguese sequence. The course expands students’ vocabulary and grammatical control while developing communication related to travel, hotels, housing, city navigation, routines, comparisons, and past experiences.

The modules feature Angola, Equatorial Guinea, Cape Verde, Guinea-Bissau, East Timor, and other Portuguese-speaking contexts. Students complete practical writing tasks, oral simulations, descriptive presentations, cultural reflections, and a final audio guide for new students.
