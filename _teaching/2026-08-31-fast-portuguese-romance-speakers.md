This accelerated course draws on students’ knowledge of Spanish and other Romance languages to develop foundational Portuguese proficiency within one semester. Taught primarily in Portuguese, it emphasizes communication, pronunciation, grammar, and intercultural understanding.

Grounded in project-based and socially responsive pedagogy, the course incorporates authentic literature, music, films, role-play, collaborative conversation, comparative language analysis, and digital projects. Students explore the pluricentric Portuguese-speaking world while developing practical speaking, listening, reading, and writing abilities.
