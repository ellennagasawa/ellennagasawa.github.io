Advanced Portuguese I develops fluency, accuracy, and confidence in speaking, writing, reading, and listening. Students engage with literature, journalism, film, music, and current issues from Brazil and the broader Lusophone world.

The course incorporates debates, literary analysis, creative writing, collaborative projects, and a final research presentation. A major literary component uses *Torto Arado*, by Itamar Vieira Junior, to connect language development with questions of identity, land, culture, history, and social justice.
