I authored and taught this English for Specific Purposes course (as well as English Applied to International Relations I, II and III) for undergraduate students in International Relations. Students used English to examine the United Nations, human rights, diplomacy, international cooperation, and contemporary global issues.

The course developed academic and professional communication through critical reviews, executive summaries, oral presentations, résumés, cover letters, and simulations of the English-language examination used in Brazil’s diplomatic-service selection process. The final project integrated research, professional writing, and an oral presentation of an international cooperation proposal.
