I coauthored and co-designed this 60-hour online course with Graziela Hoerbe Andrighetti. The course introduces the field of Portuguese as an Additional Language and examines its development in Brazil, relevant terminology, learner profiles, professional contexts, and possibilities for teaching and research.

Students investigate language policies and pedagogical practices involving Deaf and Indigenous communities, migrants and refugees, heritage-language learners, and other multilingual populations. Assessments include a conceptual map, an infographic on language policy, a critical forum contribution, and the design of a pedagogical activity for Portuguese as a Language of Welcoming.
