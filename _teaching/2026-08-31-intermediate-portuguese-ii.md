Intermediate Portuguese II strengthens oral communication, academic writing, reading comprehension, grammatical accuracy, and intercultural competence. Students work with literary, journalistic, artistic, and audiovisual materials from Portuguese-speaking communities.

The course includes process writing and revision, student-led discussions, music and news presentations, debates, collaborative language games, and a final research project. The study of Machado de Assis’s *O Alienista* connects literary interpretation with critical discussion, vocabulary development, and oral and written argumentation.
