I taught Intermediate Spanish II as part of Georgetown University’s coordinated Intermediate Spanish Program. The course developed oral communication, reading, writing, listening, grammar, and intercultural competence through collaborative tasks, debates, compositions, film, and authentic cultural materials.

Major themes included innovation, human rights, business, sustainable development, and the environment. Students completed process-based writing assignments, oral examinations, debates, cultural comparisons, and projects connected with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
