I contributed to the design and facilitation of this graduate seminar with Professor Margarete Schlatter and doctoral colleague Kaiane Mendel. The course examined sociocognitive perspectives on reading, definitions of reading proficiency, qualitative and quantitative parameters of textual complexity, and the relationship among readers, texts, and tasks.

Students analyzed reading materials and proficiency assessments, including Celpe-Bras, Prova Brasil, graded readers, and literacy resources. The seminar connected theoretical readings with assessment analysis, classroom practice, and participants’ ongoing research.
