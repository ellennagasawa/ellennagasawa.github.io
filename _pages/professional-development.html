Teacher Education and Curriculum Development

LXP Author Development — Formação de Autores LXP

Universidade do Vale do Rio dos Sinos — UNISINOS, Brazil
University Extension Course | 8 hours | 2023

This formal professional-development course strengthened my preparation as an author and designer of digital learning experiences. The training addressed the planning and production of multiple types of instructional content, including written materials, presentations, interactive resources, audio, and video.

I developed skills in selecting the most appropriate media and content format for different pedagogical goals; organizing and writing instructional materials for online learning; preparing scripts and recording plans; and producing audio and video resources in a professional studio environment. The course also addressed recording preparation, communication for the camera and microphone, and the alignment of multimedia resources with learning objectives, learner needs, and course structure.

This experience directly informed my subsequent work designing modular, asynchronous, and hybrid courses and creating multimodal instructional materials for digital learning platforms.
