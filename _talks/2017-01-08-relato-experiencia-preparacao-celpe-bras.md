This presentation reported on teaching practices developed in the Portuguese for Foreigners Program at UFRGS for learners preparing for Celpe-Bras.

The session addressed integrated reading-and-writing tasks, communicative genres, instructional materials, feedback, and the relationship between classroom practice and the examination construct.
