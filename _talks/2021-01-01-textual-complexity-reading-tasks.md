I coauthored this conference contribution with Margarete Schlatter and Kaiane Mendel. The presentation was delivered by coauthors.

The study examined relationships among textual complexity, reading proficiency, task demands, and the pedagogical selection of texts and activities for Portuguese as an Additional Language.
