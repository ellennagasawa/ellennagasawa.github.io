This presentation reflected on a teaching practicum in UFRGS's Portuguese for Foreigners Program focused on Celpe-Bras preparation.

The account discussed lesson planning, materials development, classroom interaction, integrated reading and writing, learner support, and the professional learning generated through supervised teaching.
