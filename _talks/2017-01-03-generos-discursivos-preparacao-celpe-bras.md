This presentation examined the use of discourse genres in Portuguese as an Additional Language for Specific Purposes courses preparing learners for Celpe-Bras.

The session connected genre analysis, communicative context, interlocutor relationships, purposes for writing, source-text integration, and the design of pedagogical tasks.
