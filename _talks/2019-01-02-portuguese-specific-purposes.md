This presentation discussed Portuguese as an Additional Language for Specific Purposes as an approach grounded in situated language use, learner needs, and communicative goals.

The session addressed needs analysis, genre-based pedagogy, materials design, and the relationship between specific-purpose instruction and preparation for the Celpe-Bras examination.
