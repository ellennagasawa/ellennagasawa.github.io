This presentation introduced an asynchronous Portuguese course developed with artificial intelligence support.

I discussed how AI functioned as a collaborative resource during brainstorming, module organization, task design, materials creation, text revision, and Canvas integration while preserving communicative learning goals and teacher decision-making. Examples illustrated how educators can expand their instructional-design capacity without relinquishing pedagogical responsibility or professional judgment.
