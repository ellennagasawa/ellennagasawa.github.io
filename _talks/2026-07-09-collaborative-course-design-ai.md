This presentation introduced a five-week asynchronous Portuguese course developed with artificial intelligence support.

I discussed how AI functioned as a collaborative resource during brainstorming, module organization, task design, materials creation, text revision, and Canvas integration while preserving communicative learning goals and teacher decision-making.

Examples from the completed course illustrated how educators can use AI intentionally to expand their instructional-design capacity without relinquishing pedagogical responsibility, professional judgment, or critical oversight.
