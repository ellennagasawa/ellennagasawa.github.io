This presentation analyzed Portuguese as an Additional Language textbooks in relation to the specific communicative and assessment demands of Celpe-Bras preparation.

The study considered how instructional materials represent examination tasks, genres, reading-and-writing integration, language use, and opportunities for meaningful preparation.
