This hands-on workshop examined artificial intelligence as a mediating resource in instructional design rather than as a replacement for teacher expertise.

Using the development of a five-week asynchronous Portuguese course as a case study, I demonstrated how AI supported brainstorming, content organization, task design, materials development, text revision, basic HTML coding, and the integration of interactive activities into Canvas.

Participants explored strategies for writing pedagogically grounded prompts and aligning AI-supported course design with communicative, task-based, and genre-based approaches. The workshop emphasized teacher agency, curricular coherence, ethical decision-making, and critical evaluation of AI-generated materials.
