I coauthored this conference contribution with Kaiane Mendel and Gabriela Sirianni. The presentation was delivered by coauthors.

The work discussed how the Acervo Celpe-Bras supports access to examination materials and creates opportunities for pedagogical development, assessment analysis, teacher education, and research in Portuguese as an Additional Language.
