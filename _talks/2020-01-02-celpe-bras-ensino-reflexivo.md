I presented this coauthored contribution with Margarete Schlatter on the relationship between Celpe-Bras and reflective teaching in Portuguese as an Additional Language.

The session considered how the examination's construct, integrated tasks, and communicative orientation can inform pedagogical reflection, materials design, and classroom assessment.
