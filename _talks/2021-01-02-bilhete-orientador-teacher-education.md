This online presentation examined the use of the *bilhete orientador*, a written formative-feedback tool, in the education of teachers preparing candidates for the Celpe-Bras examination.

The session discussed how focused written guidance can support lesson planning, reflective practice, materials development, and the alignment of pedagogical decisions with Portuguese for Specific Purposes.
